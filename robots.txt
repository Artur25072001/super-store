# Crawling is deliberately left open. Every page carries a "noindex" directive
# in its <head>, and search engines can only honour that directive if they are
# allowed to fetch the page. Blocking the crawl instead would leave the URLs
# eligible to appear in results as "indexed, though blocked by robots.txt".
