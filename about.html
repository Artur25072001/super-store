Since 1968, in this story

Two hundred acres, one farm store.

The Lane family planted the first orchard rows on the Escarpment in 1968 — back when “farm store” meant a table by the road and a coffee tin for the money. Three generations later the table is still there, Thursday to Sunday, plus the hives, the bakery, the press house and the flower beds the market runs on.

The online shop is the newest table by the road. Everything on it is made, grown or baked on the farm — nothing is bought in and resold — and everything ordered before 11 a.m. is packed the same morning and driven out the next.

A note about this note: none of that happened, because Orchard Lane Provisions is not a real farm. It is a fictional business built as a portfolio sample by Artur Dymchevskyi to demonstrate a complete online store: catalogue, basket, promo codes, checkout with delivery and pickup, order confirmation and tracking. No payment is collected anywhere on the site.
