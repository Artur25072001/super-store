Before you ask

When does my order arrive?

Order by 11 a.m. and delivery lands the next morning, anywhere in the Niagara Region. Bread is baked Friday and Saturday mornings only, so bread orders close Thursday at 6 p.m.

What does delivery cost?

A flat $7.95, free on baskets over $60, and pickup at the farm stand is always free. HST is calculated at checkout so the number you approve is the number you pay.

Can I order cider and wine?

Yes — with ID. Alcohol orders are 19+ and the driver checks at the door, exactly like at the market stall. No ID, no handover, refund on the spot.

What if a jar breaks or the flowers wilt?

Tell us within a day and it is replaced in the next van or refunded, your choice. We pack jars in wood wool, but nobody is perfect — we would rather fix it than argue about it.

Is this a real store?

No — and it says so everywhere. Orchard Lane Provisions is a demo online store built as a portfolio sample by Artur Dymchevskyi. Every product, price, rating and review is fictional, and the checkout collects no real payment — the whole flow exists so you can click through a working shop.

The van leaves at 11.

Everything you put in the basket before then is packed, driven and on your step the next morning. After that, it rides with tomorrow’s run.
